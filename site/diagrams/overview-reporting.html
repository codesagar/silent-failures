What does a bar mean?

Every episode is counted once. Green includes every checker pass, whether or not the agent reported success.

For checker failures, color shows what the final answer said—or that no answer arrived.

Acknowledgment can follow many retries. Silence can also accompany a checker pass; inspect the cross-tab below.
